Contact Us

Questions, feedback, or found a bug? We would love to hear from you.

Compress To KB is a small, independent project built to give everyone a free and genuinely private way to compress images. If something is not working as expected, if you have a feature request, or if you just want to say hello, reach out any time.

Email

The fastest way to reach us is by email at contact@compressortokb.com. We read every message and try to reply within 1–2 business days.

Reporting a Bug

Since every compression happens locally in your browser, bugs can sometimes be specific to a particular browser, device, or image. When reporting an issue, it helps a lot if you can include:

  • The browser and device you were using (e.g. Chrome on Android, Safari on iPhone)
  • The image format you uploaded (JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, etc.)
  • The target size you selected
  • Any error message shown on screen

Feature Requests & Feedback

Have an idea for a new target size, an output format, or a feature that would make the tool more useful? We genuinely welcome suggestions and read all of them, even if we cannot reply to every message individually.

Business & Partnerships

For advertising, partnership, or press inquiries, please use the same email address above with a short description of your request.
