How It Works

Four simple steps to compress your images without compromising privacy. All processing happens locally in your browser.

1. Upload Your Image

Drag and drop any JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, GIF, BMP, or TIFF file into the drop zone, or click to browse your files. Supports images up to 50MB with full EXIF orientation support.

2. Choose Target Size

Select your desired output size from 20KB to 500KB. Our intelligent binary search algorithm automatically adjusts quality and dimensions to reach your target precisely.

Supported Image Formats

Our compressor works with all major image formats and intelligently selects the best output format for your needs.

JPG / JPEG

The most common photo format. Excellent compression for photographs with rich colors. Our tool optimizes quality settings to reach your target size while preserving visual fidelity.

PNG

Lossless format with full transparency support. Ideal for screenshots, graphics, logos, and images with text. We preserve transparency when compressing PNG files automatically.

WebP

Modern format from Google offering superior compression. WebP typically produces files 25-35% smaller than JPEG at equivalent quality. We auto-convert when your browser supports it.

AVIF

The next-generation format with the best compression ratios. AVIF can reduce file sizes by 50% compared to JPEG. Automatically used for very small target sizes when your browser supports it.

Your Privacy Matters

Unlike most online image compressors, we never upload your images to a server. All compression happens locally in your browser.

Every image you compress using Compress To KB is processed entirely within your web browser using JavaScript and the HTML5 Canvas API. This means your images are never sent over the internet, never stored on any remote server, and cannot be accessed by us or any third party. Compressed images exist only in your browser's memory and are discarded when you close the page.

This makes Compress To KB ideal for compressing sensitive photos, confidential documents, or any image you prefer to keep private. Your data stays on your device, exactly where it belongs. Once the page is loaded, you can even compress images without an internet connection.
